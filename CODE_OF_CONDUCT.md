# Community conduct

PyForge discussions and contributions should be respectful, constructive and welcoming regardless of experience, identity or background.

- Critique ideas and code, not people. Be specific, patient and willing to correct mistakes.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others or share another person's private information.
- Keep discussions relevant; do not spam or pressure contributors for unpaid work.
- Follow repository security guidance. Never post credentials or sensitive vulnerability details publicly.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation. They should apply these standards consistently and proportionately. Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through an already established private contact route; do not publish private details merely to request that route. No response-time guarantee or independent enforcement body is implied.
